Our Mission

Choosing the right high school classes can be overwhelming. At AlignEd, we help students find out exactly what classes (like AP, IB, or electives) best align with their unique personality, college ambitions, and future major. Inspired by platforms like FutureScape, we map your traits to the perfect educational pathway.
